# Strength Equipment Model Names — Group 1

Manufacturers: Life Fitness, Hammer Strength, Precor, Cybex, Nautilus, Star Trac, Body-Solid.

Every model below was seen in a manufacturer product listing / catalog page / official owner's manual title, or (where flagged) a reputable dealer catalogue. Nothing here was extrapolated from a naming pattern. Exercise mappings are my plain-language reading of the model name and the product description — they are descriptive, not verbatim manufacturer taxonomy.

Type legend: `selectorized` = pin-loaded stack · `plate-loaded` · `cable` · `smith/rack` · `bench` · `MULTI` = one machine, several distinct movements.

- **Nautilus — Inspiration (20, complete), Impact (23, complete), Instinct, Leverage, Plate Loaded, Multi-Stations, HumanSport.** All from Core Health & Fitness collection pages with stated totals that matched what I counted.
- **Cybex Eagle NX (15).** Complete — Cybex's own "fifteen pieces" claim matches the 15 live product pages I enumerated.

**Thin — usable but incomplete:**
- **Cybex VR3 and VR1.** Names are exact (taken from Cybex's own owner's-manual titles) but the lists are "what I could verify", not certified complete. VR3 almost certainly also has Seated Leg Curl, Standing Calf, Hip Abduction and Hip Adduction; I saw those model numbers only in a second-hand search snippet and left them out.
- **Cybex Prestige.** I verified 5 Prestige Strength VRS and 3 Prestige Strength Total Access machines from live product pages. The real line is larger.
- **Body-Solid Pro ClubLine Series 2.** 14 of a reported 17 stations verified. ProDual (11) and Leverage (9) are likely complete.
- **Life Fitness Signature Series.** Discontinued as a selectorized line and replaced by Insignia. I verified 10 individual Signature Series names from live Life Fitness pages; the full historical line was much larger and I would not ship a guessed version of it. Signature Series machines are still in thousands of gyms, so this is the most commercially significant gap in this document.

**Could not verify at all:**
- **Star Trac strength.** Star Trac's current manufacturer catalog is cardio only. Its strength lines now ship as Nautilus. I verified only 8 Star Trac-branded strength model names, all from dealer listings, and I refused to reconstruct the line by rebranding the Nautilus lists. Read the Star Trac section's note before you seed anything for this brand.
- **Cybex legacy Eagle (11000-series), Cybex VR2, Cybex Plate Loaded, Cybex MG 500.** Lines confirmed to exist; individual machine names not enumerated from a Cybex source.
- **Precor Nike Strength and BeaverFit** partner lines; **Precor legacy selectorized** (Icarian, C-Line, S-Line).
- **Nautilus ONE™ Line.**
- **Life Fitness Circuit Series** — pages return "Product Discontinued".

**Two blockers I hit repeatedly, for whoever picks this up next:**
1. `support.lifefitness.com` returns HTTP 403 to fetches. Those support-hub "Introduction" articles are the single best source for Cybex line rosters with model numbers (they list every model in a line with its name). Getting access to them would close the entire Cybex gap.
2. `gympart.com` and `fullcirclepadding.com` (parts catalogues organized by line and model) also 403. They would close the legacy Eagle / VR2 gap.

**One design caution for the app.** Several manufacturers ship the *same movement* under near-identical names across generations (Cybex "Eagle" vs "Eagle NX"; Body-Solid `S2CP` vs `S2CPX`; Life Fitness "Signature Series Chest Press" vs "Insignia Series Dual Axis Chest Press"). Since PRs are keyed on the machine identity, users who pick the wrong generation will silently split their history. Consider keying on manufacturer + line + movement and treating the generation as a display detail, or making the picker show the line prominently.

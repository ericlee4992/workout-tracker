## Medium

- **The whole-frame fail-closed check happens too early.** Ticket 05 says the box crop is sent, “never the whole frame” (`work-record/scanner-accuracy/issues/05-ask-ai-escalation.md:21-22`). `LabelCrop.pixelRect` rejects a raw region that already covers the frame at `WorkoutTracker/Domain/LabelCrop.swift:23-28`, but it does not reject a near-frame region whose 8% margin becomes the entire image after intersection at `WorkoutTracker/Domain/LabelCrop.swift:29-39`. The fixture proves the hole: `ScanFixture.plateRegion` is a real inset box, not the raw frame (`WorkoutTracker/Features/Gyms/ScanMachineLabelSheet.swift:666-668`), but its `(0.02, 0.04, 0.96, 0.92)` bounds plus the margin clamp to exactly the full 1600×500 image, so `LabelCrop.jpeg` re-encodes the whole fixture photo. The fixture uses a stub and therefore does not send those bytes over the network, and the normal portrait camera geometry does not currently collapse this way; nevertheless the claimed invariant and its regression proof remain incomplete. `theFrameItselfIsNeverEncoded` tests only the exact `(0, 0, 1, 1)` input (`WorkoutTrackerTests/LabelCropRenderingTests.swift:74-76`) and misses the post-margin case.

- **An exercise-proposal request can outlive Cancel or Add.** Ticket 06 inherits ticket 05’s timeout/fail-closed behavior (`work-record/scanner-accuracy/issues/06-ai-exercise-proposal.md:22-23`), but `suggestExercises` launches an unretained task at `WorkoutTracker/Features/Gyms/GymsView.swift:1011-1035`. Neither Cancel nor Add cancels or invalidates it (`WorkoutTracker/Features/Gyms/GymsView.swift:964-971`, `1038-1054`). The paid request therefore continues and its reply may mutate dead sheet state after dismissal. Add does copy only the `linkedExerciseIDs` present at the tap, so a late reply does not rewrite the model already saved.

- **The source-of-truth documents still contradict both network features.** `CLAUDE.md:3-8` makes `docs/SPEC.md` and `docs/DECISIONS.md` the durable product contract, yet `docs/SPEC.md:28` still says a scanned photo never leaves the phone and `docs/SPEC.md:76-79` still says the app is fully offline and talks only to its device. D53 records ticket 05’s crop/transcription exception (`docs/DECISIONS.md:62`) but not ticket 06 sending plate text plus the full exercise id/name/muscle-group list (`WorkoutTracker/Domain/ExerciseProposal.swift:66-86`). A cold agent cannot tell which network disclosures are actually intentional.

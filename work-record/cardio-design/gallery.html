Workout Tracker · Cardio design study

One workout. Lifting + cardio.

Three ways to organize a mixed session, plus the start, activity picker, gym cardio, outdoor GPS and finish screens. These are native SwiftUI simulator captures with sample data—not a working cardio feature.

A keeps both sections in the workout log. B gives the current activity its own focused view. C shows the order of activities. The large timer always belongs to the current cardio segment; the small header clock belongs to the whole workout.

Gym distance is entered from the machine in this proposal. Outdoor distance and pace would come from GPS. The map is an illustrative Central Park route, not a recording or your location. Apple Health export is a separate design decision.

# Ticket 03 — Codex round 1 (T6)

Reviewed `fb20b08..48d225c` (`ui-redesign-03`, one commit), against ticket 03, the plan's constraints, and D44/D52/D54. Inspection only, including the three committed ticket screenshots; no xcodebuild or simctl, and no source changes. Findings are ordered by severity.

- **P2 — Zone segment widths do not fit the available width.** `WorkoutTracker/Features/ActiveWorkout/WorkoutFinishedSheet.swift:297` (Spec/layout). The ticket calls for each zone's share in a stacked bar, but independently applying `max(4, W * share - 2)` does not budget the minimum widths or the HStack gaps. At W = 300 pt, durations `[3599, 1]` produce `297.9167 + 2 + 4 = 303.9167` pt; `[3595, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1]` produces 327.5833 pt, extending beyond even the card's 16 pt trailing padding. One positive zone produces W − 2 despite having no gap. Reserve `(present.count - 1) * 2` for gaps, then allocate segment widths within the remainder, redistributing space if retaining a minimum width. Include one-zone and one-second-segment cases in the correction's verification.

- **P3 — The receipt scroll loop can leave the chart screenshot cut off.** `WorkoutTrackerUITests/HeartRateSummaryUITests.swift:45` (Spec/verification). `chart.exists` does not mean the full chart is visible. The committed `03-finish-heart-rate.png` demonstrates the problem: the screenshot ends near the chart's top, omitting its clock axis and average caption. The bounded loop is reasonable for an existence assertion, but does not establish the screenshot surface promised by the ticket. Scroll the receipt list until the chart and average caption lie within the visible viewport, with a bounded failure condition; ensure View in History is reachable afterward. Merely changing to `isHittable` would still allow a partially visible chart.

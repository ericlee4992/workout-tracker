## Standards

1. **P2 — 08d #1 remains open: the new cleanup cadence still misses the 30-day deadline.** **Locations:** `server/README.md:161–164`; `server/scripts/feedback.mjs:70–77`; `server/src/pages.ts:109–111,133`. Running `prune` at least every 30 days does not delete each download within 30 days. A download made on day 0 survives a run on day 29, then is removed at the next compliant run on day 59. The strict `< cutoff` comparison also retains a file exactly 30 days old. I reproduced both outcomes by evaluating the actual `pruneDownloads` function with an in-memory filesystem, clock and database stub; no real files or services were touched. Age pruning now correctly runs before the database query, and unexpired files correctly survive a failed liveness query, but these fixes do not establish the promised maximum retention. **Suggested correction:** require deletion by each file's deadline, with an operating procedure/threshold that actually guarantees it. For example, delete all downloaded copies during a cleanup performed no more than 30 days apart, or arrange per-download deletion deadlines. Use an inclusive expiry boundary and add boundary/staggered-download coverage. Merely scheduling the existing age-based prune monthly is insufficient.

2. **P2 — 08d #2 remains partially open: restore reconciliation misses provably deleted, unexpired sessions.** **Locations:** `server/README.md:136–146`; `server/src/pages.ts:139–140`; `server/src/store.ts:83–98,121–122`. The revised promise is narrower—deleted data may return, but everything identifiable as deleted will be removed again. The procedure still deletes only **expired** sessions. A session present in the backup, successfully signed out afterward, and absent from the preserved current database is an identifiable deletion even if its expiry is weeks away. The procedure retains it, and `accountForToken` will accept its bearer token again after restore. Email about missing evidence does not cover this case: the evidence exists. **Suggested correction:** remove restored sessions absent from the evidence, or invalidate all restored sessions. Preserve a stable reconciliation boundary by stopping relevant mutations or replaying changes after the evidence snapshot; the AI off-switch alone leaves account deletion, sign-out and profile edits active (`server/src/index.ts:136–178`). The added evidence export, profile reconciliation and retention reruns are improvements. The new policy acknowledges reappearance, so the former absolute “first” promise is no longer the wording under review; its proposed relaxation must nevertheless be recorded correctly (finding 8).
